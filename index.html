match.tf has now shut down

Thank you to all the players & organisers who made match.tf home for over 6 years

A special thank you goes out to 200, the original creator of match.tf

EssentialsTF aims to continue supporting independent TF2 events & tournaments in new ways in the future


Regarding phishing scams

If you have received a message on Steam containing a link to a fake match.tf website and you are asked to "vote" for someone's team, do not enter your Steam login details.

The fake website is a phishing scam attempt to obtain your Steam credentials. We highly recommend that you change your Steam password if you have ever been to one of these sites and typed in your password.

'https://match.tf' is the only legitimate version of match.tf that has ever existed.
